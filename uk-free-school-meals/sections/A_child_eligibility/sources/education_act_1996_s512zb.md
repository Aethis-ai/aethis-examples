# Education Act 1996 — Section 512ZB (extract)

## Section 512ZB — Registered pupils in England

Verbatim extract from the Education Act 1996, section 512ZB (Provision of
free school lunches and milk), as published by The National Archives at
legislation.gov.uk. Reproduced under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

(4A) A person is within this subsection if the person—

(a) is a registered pupil at a maintained school or pupil referral unit in
England, and

(b) is in reception, year 1, year 2 or any other prescribed year group at
the school.
